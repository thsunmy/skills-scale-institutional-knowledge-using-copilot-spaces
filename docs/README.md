# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory consolidates the core project management guidance for OctoAcme so teams can find the right process, roles, and operating rhythm in one place. The documents below are designed to support onboarding, execution, stakeholder communication, and continuous improvement across cross-functional work.

## Brief overview of OctoAcme project management processes

OctoAcme’s project management approach follows a clear lifecycle from initiation to delivery and retrospective. Work begins with project initiation, where teams validate the problem, align stakeholders, and document a one-pager with goals, success metrics, milestones, risks, and resource needs. Once the initiative is approved, the team moves into planning, where scope is broken into a prioritized backlog, estimates are assigned, acceptance criteria are clarified, and the path to release is mapped. This creates a structured foundation for delivery and helps ensure that work is aligned to measurable outcomes.

The framework emphasizes clear roles and shared accountability. Product and project leaders set strategy and coordination, developers implement and test the work, QA validates quality and acceptance criteria, and stakeholders provide business context and approval. Teams communicate through daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, milestone reviews, and stakeholder updates so progress, blockers, and dependencies stay visible. This rhythm supports iterative delivery and helps teams address risks early rather than waiting until they escalate.

Quality assurance is treated as a built-in part of execution rather than a final check. The process calls for unit and integration testing where appropriate, smoke tests for critical user flows, security scanning in CI, and manual QA for feature acceptance when needed. Release work also includes pre-release validation, deployment checklists, rollback planning, and post-deploy verification to reduce operational risk. By combining disciplined execution with transparent communication, OctoAcme helps teams release changes with greater confidence and visibility.

The operating model also includes structured risk management, stakeholder communication, and continuous improvement. Teams maintain risk registers, escalate blockers through defined paths, and use regular status updates and incident communication templates to keep stakeholders informed. After each sprint, release, or major milestone, teams capture lessons learned, assign action items, and track improvements in the backlog. This creates a repeatable process for learning and adapting while keeping delivery accountable and aligned to customer value.
